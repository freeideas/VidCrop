Which file?

Mac (Apple Silicon): the .dmg. Windows: the setup.exe. Linux: the .AppImage runs anywhere; the .deb is for Debian and Ubuntu.

"Unidentified developer" warnings

VidCrop isn't paying Apple or Microsoft for a signing certificate yet, so your computer will be suspicious of it. It's fine. Here's how to get past it:

Mac: open the .dmg and drag VidCrop to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS refuses. Go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.

Windows: if you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

Linux: make the AppImage runnable first: chmod +x VidCrop-*.AppImage.

Screen recording

The first time you press Record, your computer asks for permission to record the screen. On a Mac, allow VidCrop in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Screen Recording, then restart VidCrop.
